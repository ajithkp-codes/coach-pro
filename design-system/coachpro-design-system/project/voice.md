# Voice & tone

CoachPro talks like a sharp, supportive head coach: direct, warm, and never hyped. We respect that the user is a professional with limited time and many clients. Say the useful thing, then get out of the way.

**Person.** Address the coach as **you**; refer to their clients by name or as "clients." CoachPro is never "I" — the product doesn't have a personality to perform. Avoid "we" except in help and legal copy.

**Casing.** Sentence case everywhere — buttons, menus, titles, table headers. The only uppercase is the `overline` eyebrow style. Never Title Case UI, never ALL CAPS for emphasis (use weight or `accent-text`).

**Tone by moment.** Default is calm and factual ("4 of 5 sessions logged"). Celebrate progress plainly, not loudly ("Best week yet" beats "🎉 Amazing job!!!"). Deliver risk without alarm ("Adherence dropping — 2 missed check-ins"). Errors take responsibility and give the next step ("Couldn't save the program. Check your connection and try again").

**Numbers.** Lead with the number when it's the point ("92% adherence"), round for humans (14,820 not 14,819.6), and always attach a unit or timeframe ("+3 kg this month," not "+3"). Set them in the `Metrics` or `data` styles.

**Emoji.** Not in the product UI. Fine, sparingly, in lifecycle email and celebratory push if it fits the moment — one, never a row.

**Words we use:** client, program, session, check-in, adherence, trend, on track / at risk / off track, streak, baseline, breakthrough.
**Words we avoid:** user, crush it, beast mode, grind, no pain no gain, journey (overused), leverage, utilize, seamless, revolutionary.

**Examples.**
Empty state — *"No clients yet. Add your first to start tracking their progress."*
On-track nudge — *"Maya's on a 6-week streak. A quick note goes a long way."*
At-risk nudge — *"Darnell has missed 2 check-ins. Reach out?"*
Button — *"Assign program"*, *"Send check-in"*, *"Log session"* — verb + noun, sentence case.
