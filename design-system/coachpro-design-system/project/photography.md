# Imagery & graphics

CoachPro's imagery is **high-contrast, grainy black-and-white training photography** — motion, effort and focus caught honestly, with visible grain and natural blur. Think of the body in work, not posed stock fitness. The interface and the vermilion sit *on top* of this monochrome world; photos themselves stay monochrome so the brand color always reads as the signal.

**Treatment.** Convert to rich black-and-white with deep `ink`-level shadows and controlled highlights; keep (or add) fine grain. For text or UI over a photo, darken the crop with an `overlay` scrim and set type in `ink-inverse`. The vermilion mark goes on photography only via the `paper` version or on a sufficiently dark crop — never vermilion-on-mid-grey.

**Crops & shape.** Lean into the arc: crop with a quarter-circle or a `radius-lg`/`radius-xl` corner, or let a `ProgressRing` frame a portrait. One strong image beats a collage.

**Data as imagery.** On dashboards and the site, charts and the progress ring *are* the hero graphics. Draw them in `ink` and `brand` on `surface`/`surface-sunken`, with `mono` tick labels — the instrument-panel look. Keep chart ink to one accent plus neutrals; bring in `pos`/`warn`/`neg` only to mark status, always with a label.

**Avoid.** Saturated gym stock, lens-flare hero shots, multiple accent colors in one graphic, drop-shadowed cut-out people, and decorative 3D blobs or gradients. If an image could belong to any fitness app, it isn't ours.
